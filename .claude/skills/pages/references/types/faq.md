<page_type name="faq" mode="retrieval" goals="answer and route" nav="full">

<job>
Real questions customers ask, answered directly, each leading to the page with more detail. Questions
belong first on the pages where they come up: service, pricing, landing and location pages. A page of
their own is worth making only when there are many real ones, about ten or more, across several
topics. Made-up questions cost trust. FAQ rich results are no longer shown in Google search, so this
page is for readers only.
</job>

<reader>
Someone with one specific question, usually close to buying or already a customer. They scan for
their question and leave once it is answered.
</reader>

<exists>
Only questions `public/` and `brand/` shows customers asking, or that the company answers in its own
text. A "Why choose us?" question is marketing and is cut. With fewer than about ten, the
questions go on the pages they belong to instead.
</exists>

<inventory>
  questions   each question in a customer's words, and where it came from
  answers     the company's own answer, and the page that says more
  topics      the groups the questions fall into (booking, cost, the work, after)
</inventory>

<select>
  - The company's own answers, shortened, beat anything written for them.
  - The answer comes first, in a sentence or two; the detail is a link.
  - Group by topic when there are more than about eight.
</select>

<sections>
In page order. `core` is on nearly every FAQ page; everything else is a choice made against the
material. Each line says what the band holds, then when it earns its place.

  topics      optional  The topics, linked to their groups. When: more than about eight questions.
  questions   core      Each question in a customer's words, with its direct answer and a link on.
  still stuck  core      How to ask something else: phone, email, the contact page.
</sections>

<phrase>
  - Questions are written the way a customer would ask them.
  - Answers open with the answer: yes, no, a number, a name.
  - Everything in this file describes a shape. Never reuse its wording, or another page's.
</phrase>

<show>
  - No photographs. A picture only where it identifies what an answer refers to.
</show>

<links>
From: the footer, and the pages whose questions it gathers. To: the detail page for each answer.
</links>

</page_type>
