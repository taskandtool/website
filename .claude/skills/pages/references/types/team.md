<page_type name="team" mode="persuasion" goals="credibility transfer" nav="full">

<job>
The people a customer will deal with, and why they are qualified. Where the person is what is bought
(a lawyer, a dentist, an accountant, a therapist, a consultant), this page carries the decision.
Where the crew is what is bought, a band on the about page is usually enough. A team page with
placeholders, missing photographs or people who have left is worse than none.
</job>

<angle>
Decide per page and write in the brief's frontmatter, from this brand's own material.

  reader  someone choosing a person, or checking who will turn up.
  pain    will this person be qualified, and will they listen.
  offer   each person's qualifications and what they handle, with a face.
  lead    usually the people themselves, grouped the way a reader chooses between them.
</angle>

<exists>
Make a team page when there are more than about four people, or when customers choose a person.
Otherwise the people go in the about page's people band. Leave out anyone `public/` and `brand/` have no
role for.
</exists>

<inventory>
  people      name, role, photograph, as the brand gives them
  credentials qualifications, licences, board certification, bar admissions, memberships
  handles     what each person does or treats, named as the services are
  more        languages, education, publications, whether they take new clients, a personal line
  proof       reviews that name them, with the role the reviewer gives
</inventory>

<select>
  - Real photographs, all the same kind. Where some people have none, set everyone in type
    rather than mix faces with blanks.
  - What each person handles beats a CV.
  - A role comes from the brand or from a review that states it. It is never guessed.
</select>

<sections>
In page order. `core` is on nearly every team page; everything else is a choice made against the
material. Each line says what the band holds, then when it earns its place.

  intro       optional  One or two lines on who the team is. When: the page needs context.
  people      core      Each person: photograph, name, role, qualifications, what they handle, link.
  groups      optional  The people grouped by what they do or where they work.
                        When: there are enough people that a reader must choose.
  proof       optional  Reviews that name people. When: they exist.
  action      core      Book with or contact a person, or the company.
</sections>

<phrase>
  - People are named as the brand names them. Qualifications in the form a customer recognises.
  - A personal line only where the brand's own material has one.
  - Everything in this file describes a shape. Never reuse its wording, or another page's.
</phrase>

<show>
  - Portraits of the actual people, taken the same way. Never stock, never silhouettes.
</show>

<links>
To: each person's page, where there is one; their services; booking. From: the about page and service pages.
</links>

<variant name="person" label="one person's page" mode="persuasion">
Photograph, name, role; qualifications and licences with numbers; what they handle, linked to those
services; optionally languages, education, publications and whether they take new clients; reviews
naming them; and booking with this person. Stay within the rules for the profession (industries.md).
</variant>

<industry>
Healthcare: board certification, languages, whether they accept new patients, the insurance they
take. Law: admissions and practice areas, and never implied results. Trades: the crew and the licence
holder. Agencies: who will actually do the work.
</industry>

</page_type>
