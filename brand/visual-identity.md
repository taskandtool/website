# Visual identity

The brand's own colours, type, and logo, stated precisely enough to set the
site's theme from (`styles/theme.css`). Colours as 6-digit hex; fonts by
family name. When a Company Brain writes this file, every value is read from
the real site or the owner's material and cited.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Where it is used today |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (the one action colour) | to fill | to fill |
| Dark | to fill | to fill |
| Light | to fill | to fill |
| Neutral (secondary text) | to fill | to fill |
| Others | to fill | to fill |

## Type

- **Display (headings):** to fill — family, weights, where it is licensed from (Google Fonts, a file, a system font)
- **Body (reading):** to fill

## Logo

- Files in `logo/` (svg preferred): to fill
- Clear space, minimum size, dark and light versions: to fill
