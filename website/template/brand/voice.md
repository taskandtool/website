# Voice

The voice card for everything written on this site. The `writing` skill uses
it before drafting a page; fill it in from the owner's own words, the brain's
`brand/` notes when there is a Company Brain in this project, or a captured
site. Lines marked *to fill* are placeholders, not decisions.

```text
VOICE CARD
reader:        to fill — the actual person who lands here and what they need
speaker:       to fill — who is talking (the owner, the team, the company)
relationship:  to fill — peer | guide | operator | expert | host
register:      plain
cadence:       short sentences, one thought each; no fragments for effect
vocabulary:    to fill — the nouns and verbs the business actually uses
proof style:   to fill — demonstration | numbers | examples | process | credentials
humor:         none unless the owner uses it
taboos:        invented customers, results, awards, prices; hype words; "welcome to"
primary action: to fill — the verb and object of the one thing a visitor should do
```
